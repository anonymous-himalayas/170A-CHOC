The Data Dictionary below is described to the best of billy's ability

CHOC Data
If it ends in 365 it is the amount within the year

no_show: whether or not they showed up to their appointment 
age: patient's age
GENDER: patient's gender 
RACE: patient's race (White, Asian, Black or African American, Other)
ETHNICITY: whether they or hispanic or not (Hispanic or Latino, Not hispanic or latino)
APPT_WEEKDAY: Day of the week, Category Monday Tuesday ... Friday
APPT_DAY_PART: Time of day, Category Morning Afternoon Evening
DURATION: Minutes, Int 15 min intervals
BOOK_TO_APPT: Appointment booked X amount of days before, Int
APPT_SEQ: Appointment Type, Category Unknown (lots of it) First Appointment Follow Up
CANCELS_365: How many cancellations, Int
APPTS_365: How many appointments, Int
NOSHOWS_365: How many appointments did not show up, Int
RESCHEDS_365: How many reschedules, Int
TELEHEALTH_IND: whether appointment was online or not, Bool Yes No
MOBILITY_ABILITY_DX_IND: Are they incapable of moving (i.e. broken leg), Bool Yes No
MENTAL_HEALTH_DX_IND: Do they have mental health problems, Bool Yes No
PRIMARY_CARE_365: How many primary Care appointments did they have previously, Int
clinic_type: Appointment type, Category Specialty Primary Care
FIRST_APP_IND: Is it their first appointment, Bool Yes No
INSURANCE_TYPE: insurance type, Category Government Commercial Self Other/Unknown
PED_COMORBIDTY: Did they come for a previous chronic condition or some other reason/illness, Bool Yes No

SVI Data
If it starts with E it is an estimate, and M it is margin of error, Int
If it includes a P after E or M its percentage, Float
If it includes a L at the end it is a percentile, Float
If it starts with S then it is a Sum of the EPLs in the section, Float
If it starts with F its a flag for if they are within the 90th percentile, Bool Yes
If the line includes 1. 2. 3. 4. at the start it is under the following themes
1. Socioeconomic Status
2. Household Characteristics
3. Racial & Ethnic Minority Status
4. Housing Type/Transportation
If there is no number at the start there is not following theme

E_HU, M_HU: Estimated total number of housing units, Int
E_HH, M_HH: Estimated total number of households, Int
1. E_POV150, M_POV150, EP_POV150, MP_POV150, EPL_POV150, F_POV150: Persons below 150% poverty
1. E_UNEMP, M_UNEMP, EP_UNEMP, MP_UNEMP, EPL_UNEMP, F_UNEMP: Civilian (16+) unemployed
1. E_HBURD, M_BURD, EP_HBURD, MP_HBURD, EPL_HBURD, F_HBURD: Housing Burden with less than 75K annual income
1. E_NOHSDP, M_NOHSDP, EP_NOHSDP, MP_NOHSDP, EPL_NOHSDP, F_NOHSDP: Person (25+) No HS Degree
1. E_UNINSUR, M_UNINSUR, EP_UNINSUR, MP_UNINSUR, EPL_UNINSUR, F_UNINSUR: Uninsured Noninstitutionalized total population estimate
2. E_AGE65, M_AGE65, EP_AGE65, ME_AGE65, EPL_AGE65, F_AGE65: Persons 65 and older
2. E_AGE17, M_AGE17, EP_AGE17, ME_AGE17, EPL_AGE17, F_AGE17: Persons 17 and younger
2. E_DISABL, M_DISABL, EP_DISABL, MP_DISABL. EPL_DISABL, F_DISABL: Civilian Noninstitutionalized with disability
2. E_SNGPNT, M_SNGPNT, EP_SNGPNT, MP_SNGPNT, EPL_SNGPNT, F_SNGPNR: Single Parent Household with Children under 18
2. E_LIMENG, M_LIMENG, EP_LIMENG, MP_LIMENG, EPL_LIMENG, F_LIMENG: Persons (5+) speak English less than well
3. E_MINRTY, M_MINRTY, EP_MINRTY, ME_MINRTY, EPL_MINRTY, F_MINRTY: Not white (basically)
4. E_MUNIT, M_MUNIT, EP_MUNIT, MP_MUNIT, EPL_MUNIT, F_MUNIT: Housing in structures with 10 or more units
4. E_MOBILE, M_MOBILE, EP_MOBILE, MP_MOBILE, EPL_MOBILE, F_MOBILE: Mobile homes
4. E_CROWD, M_CROWD, EP_CROWD, MP_CROWD, EPL_CROWD, F_CROWD: More people than rooms
4. E_NOVEH, M_NOVEH, EP_NOVEH, MP_NOVEH, EPL_NOVEH, F_NOVEH: No vehicles
4. E_GROUPQ, M_GROUPQ, EP_GROUPQ, MP_GROUPQ, ELP_GROUPQ, F_GROUPQ: Persons in group quarters
1. SPL_THEME1, RPL_THEME1, F_THEME1: Sum of series for the socioeconomic status from those EPL values
2. SPL_THEME2, RPL_THEME2, F_THEME2: Sum of series for the house hold characteristics from those EPL values
3. SPL_THEME3, RPL_THEME3, F_THEME3: Sum of series for racial & ethnic minority status (just equal to ELP_MINORITY)
4. SPL_THEME4, RPL_THEME4, F_THEME4: Sum of series for housing type/transportation from those EPL values
F_TOTAL Sum of the 4 F_THEME
The rest of these variables are considered Adjunct variable (additional census data not related to the overall SDOH)
E_DAYPOP: Daytime population
E_NOINT, M_NOINT, EP_NOIT, MP_NOIT: No internet subscription
E_AFAM, M_AFAM, EP_AFAM, MP_AFAM: Count of Black/African people

E_OTHERRACE, M_OTHERRACE, EP_OTHERRACE, MP_OTHERRACE: Some other race not listed

COI Data
If it starts with z it is a z score as our weighted average, float
If it starts with c5 it is 5 categories, very low, low, moderate, high, very high
If it starts with r it is scores from 1 to 100
If it ends with nat it represents national 
If it ends with stt it represents state
If it ends with met it represents metro
There are 4 overall measurements Education Domain, Health and Environment, Social and Economic Domain, and COI which is some kind of measurement
z_ED_nat, c5_ED_nat, r_ED_nat, c5_ED_stt, r_ED_stt, c5_ED_met, r_ED_met: Education Domain Variables
z_HE_nat, c5_HE_nat, r_HE_nat, c5_HE_stt, r_HE_stt, c5_HE_met, r_HE_met: Health and Environment Variables
Z_SE_nat, c5_SE_nat, r_SE_nat, c5_SE_stt, r_SE_stt, c5_SE_met, r_SE_met: Social and Economic Domain Variables
z_COI_nat, c5_COI_nat, r_COI_nat, c5_COI_stt, r_COI_stt, c5_COI_met, r_COI_met: COI Variables
